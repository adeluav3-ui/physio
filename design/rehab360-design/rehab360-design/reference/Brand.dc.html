REHAB360 BRAND THEME

Navy for trust, green for action, blue for guidance.

Every colour below comes from the logo. Each has one job, so patients, professionals and admins learn the same visual language and still feel at home in their own dashboard.

Rules

Do

  • Put the one main action on each screen in action green.
  • Use navy for text and headings, never pure black.
  • Keep cards white on the pale ground with a 1px line.
  • Pair every status colour with a word.
  • Keep the logo on white or the pale ground; badge it on dark.

Don't

  • Put white text on bright green (only 2.5:1).
  • Use red, amber or green for decoration; they mean status.
  • Add shadows, extra gradients or emoji.
  • Use the five discipline dots as page themes.
  • Change the logo's colours or proportions.
