Questions people ask

Is this for emergencies?

No. If you have severe pain, chest pain, trouble breathing, sudden weakness or loss of feeling, go to the nearest hospital.

Are the professionals licensed?

Every professional is checked against their licensing body before they appear in search.

Who can see my records?

Only you and the professionals you choose. Every access to a record is logged.
